Associate Professor · Political Science and Diplomacy

In Tae Yoo

Dankook University — currently Visiting Scholar, Institute for International Science and Technology Policy, Elliott School of International Affairs, George Washington University (2026)

In Tae Yoo studies the intersection of international security and international political economy, with a focus on how cybersecurity, economic security, and the reconfiguration of semiconductor and defense-technology supply chains are reshaping foreign policy among the United States, South Korea, and Japan.

His work moves between quantitative analysis and qualitative policy research, and appears in both Korean- and English-language journals. During the 2026 academic year he is based at the Elliott School of International Affairs in Washington, D.C.
